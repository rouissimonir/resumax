Resumax Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Resumax ("we", "our", "the app") helps you improve and reformat your CV/résumé using AI. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how long it is kept. It applies to the Resumax mobile app and its backend service.

1. What we collect

When you upload a CV, we process:

  • The text content of your CV/résumé — extracted from the PDF you upload.
  • An optional headshot photo, if you choose to add one for a template that supports it (for example, Europass).
  • Your format and language preferences — the template you pick, your target role, experience level, and CV language, if you set them.
  • A device- or account-linked identifier used only to check your subscription status with our payments provider (see Section 3).

We do not require you to create an account to use Resumax's core features; the app can be used as a guest.

2. How we use it

Your CV text (and photo, if provided) is sent to third-party AI providers to rewrite and improve your CV's wording, and to render it into the template you selected. We do not use your CV content for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Who we share data with

Resumax relies on the following processors to function:

  • Groq — receives the extracted text of your CV to generate improved, ATS-optimized wording. See Groq's privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini (Google AI) — receives the extracted text of your CV for the same purpose, as an alternate AI provider. See Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — receives a device/account identifier and subscription status to verify Pro access and manage in-app purchases. Your CV content is never sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • [YOUR HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Render] — hosts the backend server that temporarily stores your uploaded file while it is processed (see Section 4).

We do not share your CV content with any other third party, and we do not use it to train our own models.

4. How long we keep it

Uploaded files and the improved CV data are stored temporarily on our server so you can re-download or switch templates shortly after processing. This data is automatically deleted on a rolling basis — currently within about one hour of upload. After that window, downloading an old CV from your in-app history will require re-processing it.

We do not maintain a permanent, indefinite copy of your CV content on our servers.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can use Resumax without creating an account.
  • You can delete an uploaded photo at any time from within the app.
  • Because we do not retain your CV content beyond the short processing window described above, there is normally nothing further to delete on our servers after that window passes.
  • If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

Resumax is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Payments

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and subscription status is verified through RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
