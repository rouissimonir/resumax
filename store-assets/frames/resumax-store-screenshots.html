Resumax — App Store screenshots

Each card is 430 × 932 CSS px. Capture at a device pixel ratio of 3 for the required 1290 × 2796, or run export.js. Apple rejects any upload carrying an alpha channel — flatten to RGB.

Screenshots must reflect actual in-app use (App Review guideline 2.3.3). Every pixel inside the device frames is a real capture; only the captions, glows and pills are added.
